How this works

Your webcam frames are processed entirely client-side using MediaPipe Hands — nothing is sent to a server. A fist (0 fingers) moves back a slide, an open palm (5 fingers) moves forward, and a single raised finger toggles fullscreen present mode. There's a 1 second cooldown between actions, same as the desktop version.

Browsers can't send keystrokes to native apps like PowerPoint, so gestures here drive the built-in deck on this page. Upload slide images exported from PowerPoint (File → Export → PNG) to use your real deck.

To control the actual PowerPoint desktop app from a link like this, you'd need a small local bridge app on your PC listening for commands — a separate, optional piece.
